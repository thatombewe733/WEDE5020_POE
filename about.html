About RESET Pilates Studio

A welcoming space for beginners and experienced clients.

Our Story

RESET Pilates Studio started in 2023 as a small studio with a few mats. The founder wanted to create a welcoming space where beginners and experienced clients could enjoy Pilates.

Today, RESET Pilates Studio offers mat and reformer classes across two Johannesburg locations with a team of qualified instructors.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help clients build strength, flexibility and confidence through Pilates in a welcoming environment.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a leading Pilates studio known for personal attention and quality Pilates training in Gauteng.
