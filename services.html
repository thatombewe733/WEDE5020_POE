Our Classes

We offer different Pilates classes to suit different fitness levels and goals.

Mat Pilates

Mat Pilates uses bodyweight exercises to improve core strength, posture and flexibility.

Reformer Pilates

Reformer Pilates uses a reformer machine to provide resistance during exercises.

Private Sessions

Private sessions provide one-on-one coaching based on individual goals.

How a Class Works

  1. Arrive and Warm Up – Clients begin with breathing and mobility exercises.
  2. Guided Movement – The instructor guides clients through Pilates exercises.
  3. Cool Down and Stretch – The class ends with stretching and relaxation.
